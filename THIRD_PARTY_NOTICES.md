# Third-party notices

GreekSwitch embeds or depends on the following third-party components.

## Hunspell dictionaries (embedded in the executable as hashed word lists)

Source: LibreOffice dictionaries repository, <https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries>.
The original files and README files are in `data/dictionaries/`. The executable doesn't contain
the `.dic`/`.aff` files themselves. It contains `data/lexicon/el.lex` and `en.lex`: every word
form, expanded with the affix rules and stored as 64-bit hashes (see `data/lexicon/README.md`).
These are derived works of the dictionaries and carry the same terms as their source, below.

### el_GR (Greek)

- Files: `el_GR.aff`, `el_GR.dic` (version 0.9, 14/03/2015)
- Authors: Steve Stavropoulos (elspell, <http://elspell.math.upatras.gr>), based on the el_GR
  enhanced dictionary by Evripidis Papakostas (<http://ispell.source.gr>)
- License: tri-licensed **MPL 1.1 / GPL 2.0 / LGPL 2.1**. GreekSwitch uses it under the
  **MPL 1.1**. The files are distributed unmodified, and you can get their source form from

- Full text: `data/dictionaries/README_el_GR.txt`

## Trigram models (embedded in the executable)

`data/ngrams/el.tri` and `data/ngrams/en.tri` are generated by `tools/NgramBuilder` from the
two dictionaries above, so they are derived works of them and carry the same terms.

## Word frequency tables (embedded in the executable)

`data/frequency/el.zipf` and `en.zipf` (compiled from `el.tsv`/`en.tsv`) are embedded in
GreekSwitch.Core and used by the decision engine.

- Source: wordfreq 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer and contributors, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
- License: wordfreq's **data is CC BY-SA 4.0** (its code, which GreekSwitch doesn't use, is
  Apache-2.0). The exported and compiled tables are adaptations of that data and are
  distributed under **CC BY-SA 4.0** too: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
  The rest of GreekSwitch is not affected; the tables can be extracted, used and modified
  under CC BY-SA 4.0 like their source.
- Changes: only the most frequent ~50k words per language; Greek final sigma restored; Zipf
  values rounded and keys normalised (see `data/frequency/README.md`).

## CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (embedded in the executable as a phonetic index)

`data/phonetic/en.idx` (generated by `tools/PhoneticIndexBuilder`, see `data/phonetic/README.md`)
holds the pronunciations of about 32k English words for loanword matching. The pronunciations
come from CMUdict; the word list and its frequencies come from the wordfreq tables above, so the
index is distributed under both sets of terms: CMUdict's license below for the pronunciations,
and **CC BY-SA 4.0** for the word list and frequencies.

- Source: `cmudict.dict` from https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict (commit `0f8072f8`, 2024-12-17)
- Changes: only words in the English frequency list and the en_US dictionary, at most two
  pronunciations each; ARPAbet mapped onto GreekSwitch's simplified phoneme alphabet and stored
  in binary with lookup indexes. The dictionary text itself is not shipped.
